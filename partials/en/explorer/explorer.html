Accountability model

Funds → Authority → Rules → Decisions → Outcomes → Evidence

This model is applied as an evidence boundary, not a marketing claim. Onchain movement can verify execution while later stages remain unverified when evidence is absent.

PLANNED architecture

Accountability Records

The future record model is prepared conceptually, not presented as deployed functionality. A record can connect funding, responsible authority, applicable rules, a decision reference, declared outcomes, submitted evidence and verification history.

Evidence states

DECLARED · EVIDENCE SUBMITTED · DOCUMENTED · INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED · INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE · DISPUTED

Integrity is separate

INTEGRITY CONFIRMED means a file or record matches its cryptographically registered original. It does not mean the content is true.

Role separation

Claimant submits a claim; platform structures and publishes provenance; an independent verifier may assess specific evidence. The public Explorer remains read-only.
